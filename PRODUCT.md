# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

JEE aspirants using an Android phone to practice for JEE Main and JEE Advanced.

## Product Purpose

Rankroom is a JEE practice app for browsing and answering previous-year questions, taking timed practice tests, and reviewing personal progress.

## Positioning

A focused, on-device JEE practice desk organized around exam, year, subject, and topic. This positioning is inferred from the app name, current flows, and the user's request; it is not a confirmed market claim.

## Operating Context

Students use the app for short practice sessions and longer timed mock sessions. Progress is currently stored on the device.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The app includes question search and filters, a timed mini mock, and local progress tracking. The six original sample questions remain in the demo mock only.
- A searchable text archive is bundled in `app/src/main/assets/question-bank.json`, generated by `tools/import_questions.py` from the extracted PDF pages.
- The available extracted source contains 147 PDFs; 146 contain question markers, producing 11,841 question entries. The available years are 2010–2026, not the full 2002–2026 range.
- The extracted text can omit diagrams, scanned content, and mathematical notation. Entries have not been individually verified against official papers or answer keys, so archive coverage and correctness must not be overstated.
- Source PDFs and answer keys are not bundled in the APK; verify unclear text against the original source papers.

## Evidence on Hand

- The Android project and existing Compose UI are in this repository.
- The six questions in `app/src/main/java/com/rankroom/jee/MainActivity.kt` are labeled in the UI as prototype examples. They are not evidence of a complete archive.
- Official paper and answer-key files have not yet been collected or verified in this repository.

## Product Principles

- Make it quick to reach a useful question set by exam, year, subject, and topic.
- Keep timed practice and review understandable and reliable.
- Be precise about question provenance and archive coverage.
- Keep progress useful without requiring an account; current progress is local.
